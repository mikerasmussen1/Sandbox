Auric Dynamo

Choose an entry, then spin. The three number reels are read from left to right as one number. A blank is skipped, so 10, a blank and 10 read as 1010. Charge fills a blank between two numbers. A modifier can multiply, replace, bank and add, or open a wheel. Every tenth play, Dynamo Surge pays. Opened from the lobby, each spin uses your Gold Coins or Sweeps Coins and the server decides the result.
